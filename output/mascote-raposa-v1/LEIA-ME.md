## Prompt inicial — histórico da geração rejeitada

Use case: logo-brand.
Asset type: first professional mascot concept sheet for a reusable personal visual identity; mascot development now, possible simplified logo later. No brand name.
Create one cohesive, beautiful design presentation of ONE original fox character, with three consistent renderings of that same fox: a large full-body hero, a smaller close-up portrait, and a small single-ink reduction of the full-body silhouette. This is a character sheet, not three different design options.
Canvas: landscape approximately 1536 x 1024. Warm pale ivory background, generous negative space and disciplined spacing. Large fox dominates about two thirds of the composition; two smaller studies occupy the remaining area, clearly separated by whitespace. No text, headings, captions, letterforms, numbers, border frames, swatches or watermarks.
Visual direction from the user's reference collection: warm yellow-to-orange-to-coral gradients, deep aubergine features, friendly small oval eyes, soft sculpted vector-like curves, lively oversized flowing tail. Make a fresh distinct design using that general aesthetic.
Character: an alert, curious, welcoming fox with upright triangular softly rounded ears, a subtly asymmetrical ear angle, softly projecting small muzzle, small dark aubergine nose, warm golden cheek mask made of just two clean sweeping curves, modest dark eyes with one tiny highlight each, gentle subtle smile. Elegant but approachable proportions; not baby-like, not aggressive, not a plush toy. Clean shape language with very few fur notches.
Pose: seated upright in a graceful three-quarter view, complete body visible, face turned enough toward viewer to show both eyes; compact grounded haunches, two simple front paws. An oversized plume tail wraps beside and partially around the body, rises in one confident open hook, with one distinctive golden tapered tip and a broad readable negative-space gap between tail and torso. Tail is the visual signature; keep a balanced compact silhouette suitable for future icon simplification. No globe, encircling planet, heart or letter shape.
Color: vivid orange body, golden yellow muzzle and chest, controlled coral gradient toward tail root and lower body, restrained aubergine paws and nose/eyes. Gradients are smooth within a handful of solid organic shape regions; geometry must do most of the work. Optional pale gold tail highlight is one simple shape, not numerous ribbons.
Portrait rendering must use exactly the same face, ears and cheek-mask geometry as the hero. The monochrome study must be a flat deep-aubergine simplified rendering of exactly the same full-body pose, with just a few ivory negative-space separations and no grayscale or gradients. This shows potential reducibility, not a finished corporate logo.
Style: sophisticated contemporary 2D character identity artwork, crisp vector-like edges, flat planes with controlled luminous warm gradients, excellent optical balance. No outlines, fur texture, realism, 3D effects, cast shadows, setting, landscape, accessories, clothing, props, stars, hearts or additional characters. This should feel like an inviting distinctive long-term personal mascot, adaptable to multiple unrelated projects.

## Prompt de simplificação — prancha entregue

Edit image 1, the three-view fox mascot sheet. Image 2 is a STYLE REFERENCE showing the required extreme graphic simplicity.
Keep the presentation structure: a large seated fox with a generous upright curved tail, a smaller face study, and a smaller one-color version of the same seated fox.
Redraw everything in the clean minimal flat 2D style of image 2: solid organic shapes, simple small oval dark-purple eyes with one white dot, no eyebrows or eyelashes, a tiny oval nose, only two soft cheek curves, plain pointed ears with NO inner-ear detail, plain paws with NO toe lines, a chest patch made from ONE simple golden teardrop, a tail made from ONE flowing orange shape and ONE golden tip. Orange, yellow and a restrained coral gradient only. Reduce the number of shapes drastically. Remove ALL realistic lighting, grain, glow, blur, shadow, layered fur, white whisker tufts, contour strokes and detailed anatomy.
Replace the entire atmospheric background with a completely uniform pale ivory #FFF8EE from edge to edge.
Make the monochrome study clearly visible in solid deep purple #270C46 against this ivory, with a clean ivory gap separating tail and body. Do not apply texture or gradients to this monochrome rendering.
Keep generous margins around all three views. No text, no logos, no words, no decorative additions. Professional simple friendly fox mascot, with a strong silhouette usable across many projects.
